# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in Chess-X a respectful, safe, and inclusive experience for everyone.

We are committed to building a community that welcomes people regardless of experience level, background, or identity, while remaining focused on the project's ethical and practical goals.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive in discussions
- giving and receiving feedback graciously
- focusing on technical and project goals rather than personal attacks
- helping newcomers learn without condescension
- keeping contributions aligned with the project's educational and private-use scope

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, discrimination, or abusive language
- personal attacks, trolling, or intimidation
- threats of violence or doxxing
- publishing private information without permission
- behavior that promotes cheating, abuse, or serious violations of platform policies

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align with this policy, and they may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for harmful behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to Chess-X, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community channels.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer through the repository's owner contact or the GitHub security/reporting channels for this project.

Please include as much detail as needed for maintainers to understand the situation and take appropriate action.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly. They may ask for additional information, investigate privately, and take action they determine is necessary to protect the community and the integrity of the project.
